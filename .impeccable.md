## Design Context

### Users
Entrepreneurs use this planner to make deliberate weekly tradeoffs across work that grows the business, work that keeps the business running, and personal or creative work that restores judgment. They are ambitious, time-constrained, and trying to convert limited discretionary hours into a sharper operating plan.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel calm, elegant, and high-agency. The voice should be concise and composed, with enough precision to support serious planning without sounding corporate or harsh.

### Aesthetic Direction
The current light, refined planner direction should stay. Copy should feel like an executive planning tool: polished, practical, and direct. Avoid playful language, hype, guilt, or overly casual phrasing.

### Design Principles
- Use clear founder-friendly terms for time categories.
- Make every instruction point to the next practical decision.
- Treat time as a portfolio of deliberate commitments.
- Keep feedback concise, specific, and calm.
- Support optimization without making the user feel rushed or judged.
